Since 2005, \textit{Dancing with the Stars} (DWTS) has aired 34 seasons, pairing celebrities with professional dancers in weekly competitions. The show employs a dual mechanism combining judge scores and audience votes to determine weekly eliminations, yet \textbf{audience vote tallies remain classified}. Over 34 seasons, two distinct combination methods have been utilized---the \textit{rank-based} and \textit{percentage-based} approaches---with recurring controversies where low-scoring contestants advanced through high audience support (e.g., Jerry Rice, Bobby Bones). Contestant characteristics (athletic background, entertainment industry, age, gender) and social media prominence\cite{googletrends} exert substantial influence on outcomes.

This study addresses an \textbf{incomplete information game} scenario: reconstructing the latent distribution of audience votes from observable elimination data, quantifying the differential impacts of combination methods, and engineering an improved voting protocol. Leveraging data spanning 34 seasons (2005--2026), we employ Bayesian inference, machine learning, and game-theoretic frameworks to demystify the voting ``black box''\cite{dwts_wiki}.





\subsection{Problem Description}

This problem focuses on analyzing the voting mechanism of \textit{Dancing with the Stars} (DWTS). In this competitive reality show, celebrity contestants partner with professional dancers to perform weekly routines, with outcomes determined by a combination of judge scores and audience votes. However, audience voting data remains proprietary and undisclosed, while the show has employed varying vote-combination protocols across 34 seasons. The central challenge lies in evaluating the fairness of these voting systems and proposing optimization strategies under conditions of incomplete vote observability.

Specific tasks include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Audience Vote Estimation:} Construct mathematical models to inversely infer each contestant's audience vote totals from judge scores, contestant attributes, and elimination outcomes. Assess the consistency between estimated votes and actual eliminations, while quantifying estimation uncertainty.
    
    \item \textbf{Voting Method Comparison:} Conduct comparative analysis of the two combination methods employed---rank-based and percentage-based approaches---examining result divergence across seasons. For controversial cases (e.g., Jerry Rice, Bobby Bones, who advanced despite low judge scores but high audience support), evaluate how methodological choice and judge deliberation mechanisms impact final rankings, and recommend optimal approaches for future seasons.
    
    \item \textbf{Influence Factor Analysis:} Develop models to quantify the impact of professional partner experience, celebrity age, and industry background on competition outcomes. Analyze whether these factors exhibit differential mechanisms of influence on judge scores versus audience votes.
    
    \item \textbf{New System Design:} Propose an alternative vote-combination system that enhances either ``fairness'' or entertainment value. Validate the efficacy of the proposed system through historical data simulation and provide actionable implementation recommendations for producers.
    
    \item \textbf{Report Deliverables:} Submit an analytical report not exceeding 25 pages, accompanied by a 1--2 page memorandum, summarizing the impact of vote-combination methodologies and offering decision guidance for future DWTS seasons.
\end{itemize}
