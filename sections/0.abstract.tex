\begin{abstract}

To demystify the "black box" of audience voting in \textit{Dancing with the Stars} (DWTS), we develop a comprehensive mathematical framework addressing a fundamental \textbf{constrained inverse problem}: reconstructing unobservable fan votes from judge scores and elimination outcomes across 34 seasons.

\textbf{Two-Layer Bayesian Framework.} We tackle the information bottleneck by constructing informative priors through a calibrated power-law model ($V_i \propto S_i^{1.2}$) fusing judge scores, celebrity attributes, and Google Trends data. By employing constrained optimization (SLSQP algorithm) to ensure estimates align with historical eliminations, our model yields a \textbf{91.7\% prediction accuracy} overall (\textbf{99.5\% for the percentage method}), with a median uncertainty measure of \textbf{CV = 0.117}, validating the stability of our estimation.

\textbf{Mechanism Analysis.} GBDT models coupled with SHAP decomposition reveal divergent evaluation standards: professional partner experience contributes significantly to judge scores but has a diminished impact on fan votes, where audiences prioritize celebrity industry background and charisma. Our counterfactual simulations further indicate that the rank-based method amplifies fan influence by 12.8\% relative to the percentage method in scenarios of high score density.

\textbf{The "Dramatic Arc" Voting System.} We propose an innovative phase-adaptive design that dynamically shifts judge-fan weights (62\% judge-led in early stages to 68\% fan-led in the finale) to mimic a narrative climax. Incorporating a controversy bonus mechanism, this system achieves a top-tier composite score of \textbf{0.860}. It is projected to catalyze a 47\% increase in social media engagement while maintaining an optimal 11.1\% controversy rate. Monte Carlo simulations confirm the system's robustness, retaining 89.3\% performance integrity under stochastic perturbations.

Our work delivers a \textbf{production-ready protocol} that reconciles competitive integrity with entertainment imperatives, providing quantified strategic value for reality television producers.
