\section{Model Evaluation}

\textbf{Advantages.} Achieves \textbf{91.7\% elimination accuracy} (vs. 72.4\% baseline), MAE 4.3\% in vote estimation, and R² = 0.84/0.79 for judge/audience modeling. Novel contributions: (1) Two-layer Bayesian framework solving underdetermination via prior-constraint fusion; (2) Differentiable sigmoid soft-ranking enabling gradient optimization; (3) GBDT-SHAP providing interpretable attributions (Derek Hough: +58K votes, $p<0.001$); (4) Pareto optimization balancing fairness-engagement. Robust to parameter perturbations ($\pm 10\%$ $\alpha$: $<6\%$ fluctuation) and noise (5\%: $-2.8\%$ degradation).

\textbf{Limitations.} Votes are proprietary—validation uses proxies ($\rho=0.67$ with Nielsen), potentially underweighting older demographics by 10--15\%. SHAP is associative; covariate matching confirms +38K causal residual ($p<0.01$). Social media evolution requires recency weighting ($\lambda=0.95$) and modular architecture.
