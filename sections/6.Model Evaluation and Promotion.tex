\section{Model Evaluation and Promotion}
\subsection{Model Evaluation}

\subsubsection{Advantages}

\textbf{Robust Predictive Performance.} Framework achieves \textbf{91.7\% elimination accuracy}, outperforming baselines (3-8\%) and linear regression ($<$70\%). GBDT explains \textbf{84\% judge variance, 79\% audience variance}, exceeding conventional approaches by 15-20\%. Sensitivity testing validates stability: $\alpha$ $\pm10\%$ → $<6\%$ fluctuation; 5\% noise → 5.07\% decline; cross-season $>85\%$ accuracy.

\textbf{Novel Methodological Contributions.} \textbf{Two-Layer Bayesian Framework} integrates priors (Google Trends, partners) with elimination constraints, resolving underdetermination unlike traditional maximum likelihood. \textbf{Sigmoid soft-ranking} enables differentiable SLSQP optimization where integer programming fails. \textbf{GBDT-SHAP} quantifies Derek Hough's +58K and age effects (+23,744) with statistical rigor.

\subsubsection{Limitations}

\textbf{Data Constraints.} Cannot validate absolute votes (proprietary data). \textbf{Mitigated}: triangulation via Nielsen ($\rho=0.67$), distributions match expected $\gamma_1=1.8$. Proxies may underestimate non-digital demographics by 10-15\%.

\textbf{Causal Inference.} SHAP shows correlations, not causality. \textbf{Mitigated}: covariate matching reveals elite partners retain +38K residual ($p<0.01$) controlling popularity, suggesting genuine skill effects beyond selection bias.
