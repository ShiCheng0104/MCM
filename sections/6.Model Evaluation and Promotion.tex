\section{Model Evaluation and Promotion}
\subsection{Model Evaluation}

\subsubsection{Advantages}

\textbf{Robust Predictive Performance.} Achieves \textbf{91.7\% elimination accuracy}. GBDT explains \textbf{84\%} judge variance and \textbf{79\%} audience variance. Sensitivity tests are stable ($\alpha$ $\pm 10\%$: $<6\%$ fluctuation; 5\% noise: $-5.07$ points).

\textbf{Novel Methodological Contributions.} A \textbf{two-layer Bayesian} design fuses priors with elimination constraints, while \textbf{sigmoid soft-ranking} enables differentiable SLSQP optimization. \textbf{GBDT-SHAP} provides interpretable effect sizes (e.g., Derek Hough +58K votes).

\subsubsection{Limitations}

\textbf{Data Constraints.} Votes are proprietary; validation relies on proxies. \textbf{Mitigated}: triangulation via Nielsen ($\rho=0.67$). Proxies may underweight non-digital demographics (10--15\%).

\textbf{Causal Inference.} SHAP is associative rather than causal. \textbf{Mitigated}: covariate matching shows elite partners retain a +38K residual effect ($p<0.01$) after controlling popularity.
