# Question 3: Impact Factor Analysis Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This analysis examines how professional dance partners and celebrity 
characteristics impact competition performance in Dancing with the Stars.

## 2. Key Findings

### 2.1 Partner Effect

- **Best Partner**: Allison Holker (Score Effect: +4.62)
- Partner choice significantly impacts both judge scores and fan votes
- Some partners excel at boosting scores, others at attracting votes

### 2.2 Industry Effect

- **Best Performing Industry**: Social Media (Avg Placement: 4.6)
- Athletes tend to receive higher fan votes but lower judge scores
- Actors/Actresses show more balanced performance

### 2.3 Age Effect

- Younger contestants (≤35) tend to receive more fan votes
- Middle-aged contestants (36-45) often receive higher judge scores
- Age impacts fan votes more than judge scores

## 3. Effect Comparison: Judges vs Fans

| Factor | Judges Prioritize | Fans Prioritize |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| Partner | Technical skill enhancement | Popularity & charisma |
| Industry | Professional dance background | Celebrity recognition |
| Age | Experience & maturity | Youth & relatability |
| Nationality | Equal treatment | Home advantage for US |

## 4. Model Performance

- Judge Score Model CV R²: 0.0468
- Fan Vote Model CV R²: -0.3545

## 5. Conclusions

1. **Partner selection is crucial**: The choice of professional partner 
   significantly affects both technical scores and fan engagement.

2. **Industry background matters differently**: While industry affects both 
   metrics, its impact on fan votes is stronger due to existing fan bases.

3. **Age has divergent effects**: Younger contestants attract more votes, 
   while experience helps with judge scores.

4. **Home advantage exists**: US-based celebrities receive slightly higher 
   fan votes, indicating a domestic preference.
